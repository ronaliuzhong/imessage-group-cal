## Description (4000)

Coucal finds a time that works for your whole group chat.

Connect your Google Calendar once, start Coucal in any iMessage chat, and see
when everyone is free, right inside Messages. No polls, no "what time works
for you?" threads.

HOW IT WORKS
• Start Coucal in a group chat (or with one friend) and send the invite.
• Friends tap it to join. Coucal shows the overlap: gray blocks where people
  are busy, open space where everyone's free.
• Tap or hold-and-drag on the calendar to propose a plan. It drops into the
  chat as a bubble everyone can answer: Going or Can't make it.
• Plans you're going to are added to your Google Calendar automatically.

MADE FOR GROUP CHATS
• See who's free right now, and who's busy until when.
• Day and week views, with a color for each person in the day view.
• Repeating plans: weekly dinners, study groups, game nights.
• Real places from Apple Maps, and your own color for each plan.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Coucal only ever sees when you're busy or free, never what your events are
called, where they are, or who's in them. Your busy times are shown only to
people in groups you join. You can delete your account at any time.

Coucal also works in any web browser at coucal.app, so friends without an
iPhone can join too.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Tracking: **No**, Coucal doesn't track people across other companies' apps or
websites.

Data collected (all **linked to the user**, used only for **App
Functionality**, none for tracking or advertising):

| Apple category | Data type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Name | Shown to people in your groups |
| Contact Info | Email Address | Your account (from Google sign-in) |
| Identifiers | User ID | Your Coucal account |
| User Content | Other User Content | Groups, plans (name, time, place, notes), your answers and colors |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, browsing, purchases, diagnostics,
usage data. Free/busy times are fetched from Google when a calendar is viewed
and aren't stored, so they don't count as "collected" under Apple's
definition.

**Notes for the reviewer** (paste, then fill in the account):

Coucal is mainly an iMessage app. To test it:
1. Open Messages and start a conversation (any contact, or yourself).
2. Tap the + next to the text box, then Coucal (under More).
3. Tap "Sign in with Google" and use the test account below.
4. Tap "Start Coucal in this chat" and send the invite bubble. The calendar
   shows the test account's busy and free times.
5. Tap a time on the calendar (or hold and drag) to propose a plan, and send
   it. Tap the plan bubble to answer Going; it's added to the account's
   Google Calendar in a calendar named "Coucal".
6. Account deletion: Coucal panel → ⋯ menu → Delete account (also available
   in the home-screen app and on coucal.app).

About Sign in with Apple (guideline 4.8): Coucal's sign-in is specifically to
connect the user's Google Calendar. The app's core feature reads Google
Calendar free/busy data and adds plans to the user's Google Calendar, so a
Google account is required to use it. This matches the exception for apps that
use a third-party service's sign-in to access that service's content.

Coucal only requests free/busy access (no event details), the calendar list,
and access to a calendar it creates. Privacy policy: https://coucal.app/privacy
